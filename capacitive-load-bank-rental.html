Are Capacitive Loads Threatening Your Power Reliability?

Modern power systems face leading power factor loads from capacitors, electronics, LED lighting, and renewable inverters. Untested systems can suffer voltage instability, overheating, nuisance tripping, or complete failure under these conditions.

Capacitive load banks safely simulate real leading PF loads, allowing you to validate generators, UPS, transformers, and distribution networks.

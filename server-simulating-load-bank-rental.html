Is Your Data Center at Risk?

Floor banks prove generator and UPS kW. They do not prove the white space. Heat in the aisle is not heat in the cabinet. A server-simulating load bank mounts in a 19-inch rack, draws from the rack PDU, and rejects heat front-to-back like production IT — so IST finds hot spots, blanking gaps, and A/B feed problems before the first GPU lands.

Why Rent a Server Simulating Load Bank?

True rack substitution: 19-inch, 4U–10U. Square, round, or threaded rails. Power from the same PDU outlets the servers will use.
Front-to-back heat: Cold-aisle in, hot-aisle out. Variable fans on the 17.5 kW and 20 kW units so you set ΔT, not just kW.
4 kW to 20 kW now: 4 kW, 6 kW, dual-feed 6.6 kW, 10 kW, 17.5 kW, and 20 kW.
A/B feed testing: Dual-channel 10 kW and dual-feed 6.6 kW load both PDUs — including failover under heat.
Fine steps: 0.5 kW on the 20 kW class. Discrete thermal steps on the 10 kW and 17.5 kW units so the IST script matches design load.
One-tech handling: 43–56 lb typical. Quarter-turn lock tabs. No forklift in the white space.

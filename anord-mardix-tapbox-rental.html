400A Databar & IBAR HXC Tap-Offs

Anord Mardix Tap Box Rental Solutions

Our Anord Mardix tapboxes are built for Databar UL 857 open-channel busway and IBAR HXC copper-conductor runs. The same Databar tap-off family covers 160–800A ratings. Installation is two steps: fast-fit the enclosure onto the channel, then turn the actuator. The actuator interlocks with the switching device so the breaker cannot close while contacts are being made.

  • Fits Databar (UL 857) and IBAR copper-conductor runs.
  • 400A MCCB tap-off — typical 400–630A IBAR enclosure, ~46 kg class.
  • Tool-free fast-fit mount; ~1.2 in (30 mm) mounting clearance on Databar.
  • Interlocked contact actuator — breaker cannot close during engage.
  • Case ground make-first, break-last on Databar tap-offs.
  • IP2X finger-safe Databar channel; IP55 IBAR covers in place.
  • Camlock or gland-plate outgoing for load-bank leads.
  • Rated for up to 600 V Databar operation, 50/60 Hz.
  • Supports 4-pole and 5-pole Databar configurations (100% / 150% / 200% neutral options).
  • Replacement parts and mixed-pallet shipments with load banks available — call to discuss.

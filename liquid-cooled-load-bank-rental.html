Why Rent a Liquid Cooled Load Bank?

Liquid-cooled and water-cooled load bank rental is the same fleet. Heat leaves the hall through your chilled-water or glycol loop instead of a fan wall. Use it when air-cooled banks would recirculate hot air, drown the room in noise, or skip the CDU / chiller path you actually need to prove.

Ampere rents a 1 MW class (~3.41 million BTU/h, ~284 tons) that flanges onto existing plant. Pair with air-cooled resistive or rack simulators when the job is mixed cooling.

ASHRAE S-class, in plain language

S-classes describe the coolant temperature delivered to IT (TCS), not the facility CHW. Common commissioning windows are S30–S45. Higher S-class means warmer supply and more free-cooling headroom. The rental holds a thermal load so you can walk those setpoints on your own plant — it is not a substitute for the ASHRAE handbook.

  • S20–S25 — colder TCS for sensitive or high-performance loops.
  • S30–S35 — typical chiller / CDU halls.
  • S40–S45 — tower or dry-cooler optimized.
  • S50+ — waste-heat or extreme free-cooling designs.

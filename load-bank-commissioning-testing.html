Data Center Commissioning & IST Load Bank Rental

Integrated systems testing (IST) is Level 5: pull utility, ride the generators and UPS, prove cooling and busway with a fake IT load. That is data-center commissioning load bank rental — not a generator exercise in the parking lot.

Grey space: resistive and R/X on switchgear, UPS, and generators at 480 V.
White space / AI bus: 800 VDC A3000DC (1,920 kW at 800 V) and liquid-cooled thermal load.
MV campus: A922 4–6 MW at 4.16 kV or 13.8 kV, hybrid xfmr package if you have no pad-mount.
Tap boxes: Starline T5, PDI / Eaton PowerWave, Vertiv E+I — same ticket as the banks.

NFPA 110 Generator Load Bank Testing

Annual generator load-bank testing is what you rent when monthly building load never hits 30% of nameplate. Hospitals, data halls, and campuses use a portable or trailer resistive bank so the diesel sees real kW, burns wet stack, and leaves a log the AHJ will accept.

Is Your 400Hz Power System Ready for Flight?

In aviation and aerospace, 400Hz systems power aircraft on the ground, support critical avionics, and keep airport operations running. A single failure in ground power units, frequency converters, or 400Hz UPS can ground flights, delay departures, or compromise safety.

400Hz load banks deliver the precise, high-frequency testing needed to validate performance under real aircraft loads.

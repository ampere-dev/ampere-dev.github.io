Portable & trailer

The resistive (and most DC) path.

  • Portable 100–700 kW: door, freight elevator, UPS room, marine, GPU. Cam-Lok. ~100–1,300 lb depending on frame.
  • Trailer 1–3 MW: campus generator, outdoor IST, yard pad. 480 V standard.
  • Why two packages: a 3 MW box will not fit the UPS room; ten 400 kW portables will not replace a trailer if the hall is a parking lot.
  • Network several portables when access is tight instead of forcing one trailer.

Indoor & rack

When the heat cannot leave through a fan wall.

  • Liquid-cooled 1 MW: 4" flanges to CHW or glycol. Quiet. Proves pumps, CDU, ΔT, and failover.
  • Server emulating 4–20 kW: lives in the 19-inch rail, plugs into the same C19 / C20 the servers will use, front-to-back airflow.
  • Do not put a 400 kW caster bank in the cold aisle and call it rack IST. Different copper, different ΔT, different pass/fail.

Mixed ticket

Most data-center weeks are not one class.

  • Trailer resistive on the generator + rack heaters in the white space.
  • Liquid 1 MW on the CDU + air-cooled on the leftover hall kW.
  • MV on the utility feed + LV portables downstream.
  • 800 V DC block + CHW liquid frame for the same hall.
  • Write the mix in notes. Networking is quoted per class — rack script ≠ trailer parallel ≠ 24-unit DC HMI.

Jobs this rental covers

  • Standby generators: NFPA 110 acceptance and annual tests, wet-stack burn-off at real kW, 0.8 PF when the nameplate is not unity. Portable through a door or a 1–3 MW trailer on the pad.
  • Data-center IST: LV hall kW, 19-inch rack PDU heat, liquid/CHW loop, MV utility feed, or 800 V DC bus. Mixed halls stay on one ticket.
  • UPS and batteries: Runtime, transfer, and thermal proof without putting live IT or process load on an unproven string.
  • Chiller / CDU / liquid cooling: 1 MW into the loop so pumps, ΔT, failover, and S-class setpoints are proven — not inferred from an air-cooled bank in the aisle.
  • White-space rack commissioning: Server-emulating 4–20 kW heaters on the same C19 / C20 the servers will use. Containment and A/B feeds included in notes.
  • DC plants and AI HVDC: LV DC racks and plants, plus 800 V DC blocks for SST / rack architecture. Do not substitute 480 V AC and call it done.
  • Utility and campus MV: 4.16 / 13.8 kV when a yard of LV cable is the wrong answer.
  • Power factor and harmonics: Resistive-reactive, inductive, capacitive, non-linear, or resonant when the spec is not unity PF or the current is not a sine.
  • Electronic / programmable: CC / CP / CR, pulses, and inverter-like profiles a resistor box cannot follow.
  • 400 Hz aviation and GPU: Hangar, ramp, and military ground power. 60 Hz resistive is the wrong machine.
  • Regenerative and energy-limited sites: When heat dump is not allowed or the kWh cost of a resistor bank is the constraint.
  • Microgrid, marine, lab, nuclear, hospital: Same fleet, different notes. Describe the source, bus, and test standard — a separate URL is not required to dispatch.

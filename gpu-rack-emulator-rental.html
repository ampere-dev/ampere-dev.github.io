Is the Hall Ready for Combined-Cooling GPUs?

A modern GPU does not reject all of its heat to water. The die and cold plate take the bulk. VRMs, NICs, PSUs, and the tray still dump air into the aisle. A water-only load bank proves the CDU. An air-only bank proves the CRAH. Neither is the GPU.

Ampere’s GPU rack emulator is a combined air-and-water load in one OCP / 600 mm cabinet: programmable water ΔP and heat on the TCS loop, plus a real air/resistive path on the PDU. Pair it with the 1 MW liquid-cooled frame or the 1 MW heat injector when the job is the plant, not the rack.

Why Combined Air + Water Loading?

Both heat paths at once. Water on the cold-plate loop and air in the same rack — the way a D2C GPU actually runs.
One cabinet, not two rented racks. On the combined build, typical 200 kW water + 50 kW air. 250 kW nameplate on the bus. Water-only build puts the full 250 kW into the loop.
Adjustable water ΔP. Valve tracks a published flow–pressure recipe so the CDU sees rack impedance, not a hose.
Air path is real load. Forced-air / resistive heat with controllable ΔT so CRAH, containment, and sprinklers see the leftover GPU air.
5 kW steps (1 kW on request) on water and air so UPS and busway IST see a GPU-like edge.
Drain-dry water loop. No last-site glycol souvenir for the next tenant.
Same HMI as the 1 MW heat injector so row proof and plant proof share recipes and logs.
In-row placement. Lands on the same aisle, PDU, and QD set the live GPU rack will use.

  • D2C GPU row IST: water on the QDs, air in the aisle, kW on the PDU — one rack, both paths.

  • Hybrid halls: Mixy-style combined load without parking a liquid bench and an air bench in two positions.
  • Before silicon: sprinklers, containment, and busway drop without a live GPU tray on the floor.

What “combined air + water” means here

The emulator is the facility-facing GPU: couplings, rack ΔP, water heat, air heat, and electrical kW. It is not a chip-level NVL72 twin and it is not two separate load banks taped together. Water and air are independently set so you can walk a 70/30, 80/20, or 90/10 split against the tray you are about to install.

  • A110W / A250W — water-only in an OCP-class rack.
  • A110C / A250C — combined air + water in the same envelope.
  • A500W / A1000W — plant water injectors.
  • A500C / A1000C — plant combined water + air injectors.
  • Do not treat rack → injector as a firmware bump. Current, fluid volume, and floor load change.

Is Your Power System at Risk?

Generators, UPS strings, and critical buses only prove themselves under real kW. A resistive load bank converts that kW to heat so you can run NFPA 110, wet-stack burn-off, and hall IST without putting live IT or process load on an unproven plant.

Ampere rents the same capacity classes used on U.S. jobsites: compact 100–700 kW portables that roll through a door, and 1.25–3 MW trailers for campus generators. Pair with reactive or capacitive when the nameplate is 0.8 PF.
